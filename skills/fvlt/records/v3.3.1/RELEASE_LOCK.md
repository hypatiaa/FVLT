## What v3.3.1 changes

There is one correction, adopted by saphir on 2026-10-30 ("our kernel needs to comply with the lean. I am good with that"). It came from PR #69 at `cbc595e`, merged as `d8dec14`, and its record is `kernel/successor/repairs/2026-10-30/15-g2-n6/`.
- **Lemma II.23.9a, guard G2.** The branch change $c\mapsto -c$ is no longer identified with the sign of $n_6$ or with chirality χ.
- **Rmk II.5a.** The sign of $n_6$ is the $y$-axis reflection $z\mapsto-\bar z$, in agreement with the verified Lean modules FVLTYReflectionV1 and FVLTN6YReflectionV1. Those modules are not in this repository; skeleton reports them verified.
- **Lock bookkeeping.** The VII.2a II.23.9 note, the concordance row and the release-scope list are updated.

The release metadata also changes: the title, the version paragraph, the release-scope standing sentence, the new v3.3.1 changelog comment and the footer. The v3.3.0 version record and changelog are kept as history.

There are 13 replacements against the v3.3.0 bytes in `kernel/post-v3.3.0/CORRECTIONS.json`. All 186 native identities and their order are kept. No native tag or status label changes, and no standing is upgraded.

Compared with the draft skeleton verified, the lock changes release metadata only: kernel lines 3, 5, 7, 9 and the footer (line 5566). The body text is identical.
