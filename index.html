Baseline contract (v3.2.0, as corrected in v3.3.0 and v3.3.1)

These come from the kernel front matter: "Register separation contract (v3.2.0)" and "How to read this document". Read the current wording there.

  • Atlas-general. FVLT is atlas-general. The substrate is 𝒜ℕ={Div(N):N>0}, not Div(720). The 720 material is a worked factorial-shell / 1001 complement instance (Part V).
  • Register separation. Keep these registers separate: atlas/Hasse, Vernier/phasor, harmonic exact-conductor, HHasse(N), Hharm(N), and application/tooling evidence. Shared divisor labels do not identify the spaces, and they do not imply a canonical unitary map.
  • Energy statistics.
    • Eqline is the scalar Ramanujan-line statistic. Eqfull is the full exact-conductor subspace energy.
    • v3.3.0 adds a complete-period energy scale. Unqualified Eqfull and Eqline use the sum inner product on ℤ/Nℤ. The mean powers are these values divided by N.
    • Windowed forms must declare their support, anchoring, normalization and taper policy.
  • Vernier contact.
    • v3.3.0 states it as follows. At integer s, the real-axis contact condition is t1s−t2s/n3∈ℤ. It reduces to n3∣t2s within the finite window when t1s and t2s are integers. This is narrower than the v3.2.6 wording.
    • Recovery of Div(s) is restricted to the reference specialization t2=1.
    • In v3.3.1 the hypothesis is n6≠0, and the sign of n6 is the y-axis reflection (see "What v3.3.1 changes").
  • Coprime Virasoro/Fock branch.
    • The branch (Part II / II.21–II.22) is non-normative research and history. Do not treat it as active theorem authority, and do not invoke Stone–von Neumann as established.
    • Mode counts: φ(720)=192 (paired 96), φ(1001)=720 (paired 360), φ(720720)=138240 (paired 69120).
  • Roots-of-unity register discipline (v3.1.18).
    • Phases are roots of unity first. Degrees and decimals are display annotations.
    • The Ω=2 ring statement is scoped to the constant exponent-two six-prime crystal (for example N=300302). Do not transfer it to other exponent profiles.
  • Quoting status.
    • Quote each item's own status text (its native-id tag or **Status:** line) together with the claim.
    • Report compound labels as written, for example "not-claimed", "comparator-only", "heuristic-context", "adapted-classical" and "unconditional finite witness". Do not collapse them into the historical core labels.
