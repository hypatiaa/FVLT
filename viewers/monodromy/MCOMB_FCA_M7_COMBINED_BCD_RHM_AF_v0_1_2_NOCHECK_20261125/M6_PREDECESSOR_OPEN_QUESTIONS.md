- Preserve the audited M5 package and its report; make this successor additive.
- Deliver a separate M6 sidecar, not a change to old schemas.

- Count display is a sparse table of exact values, capped at 2048 rendered entries; the record retains all computed rows. A dense modular raster is unnecessary for correct recurrence and can be a later display improvement.
- Spectral evolution is independently checked numerically in the complex lane only; no modular-count Fourier badge is displayed.

- Signed-representative bounds are an additional comparison. Never silently replace the integer lift underlying an old result.
- Contact sweep, M7, torsion lattices, root numbers and point-placement coverage are outside this build.

Standalone file use and localhost browser use are both tested. Independent cold audit, seating and lock remain separate.
