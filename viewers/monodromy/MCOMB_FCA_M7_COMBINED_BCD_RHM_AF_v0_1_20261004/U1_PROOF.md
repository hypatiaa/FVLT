# U1 enclosure proof and conventions

All endpoints are integers divided by 2^192. Addition/subtraction are exact; multiplication and division use explicit floor/ceil of integer quotients. Square-root lower bounds use floor(sqrt(n*2^192)); the upper bound adds one unless the square is exact. The denominator interval must exclude zero; an unresolved sign is UNAVAILABLE. No Float64 operation contributes an endpoint. Floats occur only in geometry derived from verified intervals.

## Exact model and roots

For general a1,a2,a3,a4,a6, complete the square and depress the cubic: X=x+b2/12, Y=y+(a1*x+a3)/2, A=-c4/48, B=-c6/864. Then Y^2=X^3+A*X+B and the invariant differential is dX/(2Y). These rational operations use the pinned inherited exact engine.

A strict Cauchy bound 2+ceil(max(|A|,|B|)) brackets every real root. If Delta>0, the critical points plus/minus sqrt(-A/3) separate three sign-change intervals; exact polynomial signs at dyadic endpoints establish each bracket. If Delta<0, there is one real root. Bisection preserves each sign-change bracket; an exact zero is a singleton. Roots are increasing in the output. Degenerate or unresolved brackets refuse rather than guess.

## Complete real elliptic integrals

Write K(m)=integral_0^(pi/2) (1-m*sin(t)^2)^(-1/2) dt with 0<=m<1. Starting a=1,b=sqrt(1-m), the arithmetic/geometric iteration has b_n <= AGM <= a_n. Outward interval evaluation preserves these inequalities, so K(m)=pi/(2*AGM) lies between pi/(2*a_n) and pi/(2*b_n). Termination is on enclosure width, not agreement of floating decimals. Pi uses 16*atan(1/5)-4*atan(1/239); alternating series enclose each arctangent with the first omitted term. The implemented 180 terms are smaller than the dyadic rounding contribution. Unresolved parameters or capped iterations give UNAVAILABLE.

For Delta>0, let e1>e2>e3 be the three roots, h=e1-e3, m=(e2-e3)/h. Full periods for dX/(2Y) are omega1=2*K(m)/sqrt(h) and omega2=2*i*K(1-m)/sqrt(h). Substitution X=e3+h/sin(t)^2 gives the real half-period integral. The reflected cubic supplies the imaginary cycle. Conjugation fixes omega1 and negates omega2.

For Delta<0, let r be the real root, h=sqrt(3*r^2+A)>0, m=(1-3*r/(2*h))/2. Factoring at r and substituting X=r+h*t^2 reduces the real cycle to

omega1=(2/sqrt(h))*integral_0^infinity dt/sqrt(t^4+(3*r/h)*t^2+1)=2*K(m)/sqrt(h).

The substitution t=tan(theta), followed by the double-angle formula, proves the last equality. The reflected cubic X^3+A*X-B has root -r and parameter 1-m. Its primitive real period is 2*K(1-m)/sqrt(h); multiplication by i gives the primitive purely imaginary cycle. Since the real locus is connected, the real-adapted full lattice is generated by omega1 and (omega1+i*omega_reflected)/2. Thus omega2=omega1/2+i*K(1-m)/sqrt(h), and conjugation sends omega2 to omega1-omega2. This also covers A>0,B=0 without a second algorithm.

The topological lattice conventions agree with NIST DLMF section 23.5: https://dlmf.nist.gov/23.5 . The real cycle includes one or two components as Delta<0 or Delta>0. Hence positive total real measure is Omega=omega1 or 2*omega1, respectively. These are full periods, not DLMF half-period symbols.

## Normalization and marking

The signed-response consumer binds the U1 request to the current T4 model, packet, normalization, marking, source and linked context. Computation uses that final model differential. A complete current T4 result establishes Neron normalization up to sign; a partial result establishes only model periods. T4's exact transport supplies the selected multiplier. Multiplying the enclosed model basis by that rational gives the selected basis; the positive Neron Omega does not inherit a minus sign. A nonminimal input must agree after the exact absolute-u period scaling.

For basis rows W, the marked rows are M*W, M in SL2(Z). Addresses therefore use M^(-transpose). Component labels refer to the real-adapted basis, not a coordinate in an arbitrary marked basis. These operations are exact rational matrix operations with outward interval products.

## Replay and evidence

The producer supplies no authority. The separately callable gate strictly parses bounded JSON, reconstructs the entire output from the exact request, and compares every field, enclosure, convention and source hash. In the viewer it also requires byte-canonical equality with a request independently rebuilt from the current transport and signed T4 result. Standalone mathematical replay does not establish a Neron normalization claim by itself. Unknown fields, duplicate keys, substitutions and any modified interval are rejected. U1 failure does not falsify an independently valid T4/PT1 result.

Caps: inherited canonical rational parsing; input coefficient numerators and denominators <=2^80; short |A|<=2^40, |B|<=2^60; marking entries <=4096; root bisection <=260 steps; AGM <=80 iterations; output interval width <=(1+max absolute endpoint)*2^-80; input record <=2 MB. Supported family fixtures must pass. Wider or harder cases can explicitly refuse.

Independent Python evaluation uses high-precision quadrature on the original and reflected cubic, without importing production period formulas. It is a cross-check, not the enclosure proof. Printed PARI references are checked only against their rounding intervals.

## Shared interval integration primitive for P1

The dyadic library also encloses integrals of Q(t)^(-1/2) on bounded real intervals. It rescales the interval to [0,1], subdivides, and expands around each center c. On a complex circle of radius R=4h it verifies sum_(j>=1)|q_j|R^j <= q_0/2. Therefore Q has no zero in the disk, the positive inverse-square-root branch is analytic there, and its magnitude is at most sqrt(2/q_0_lower). Taylor coefficients satisfy 2Q*f'+Q'*f=0 and are enclosed by interval arithmetic. The first 64 coefficients are integrated exactly as polynomials. Cauchy's estimate bounds the integrated omitted tail by 2h*M*(1/4)^64/(1-1/4). The proof uses complex analyticity for a real integral; it does not implement a general complex elliptic logarithm. Maximum 2048 subdivisions and depth 18; cap exhaustion is UNAVAILABLE.
