## Verified p=3 note for T3

Fable's observation is correct. With c4 a unit at 3, b2 is a unit because c4=b2² mod 3. Also -c6=b2³ mod 3. After centering the nodal point, the tangent quadratic discriminant is b2+12r=b2 mod 3. Thus -c6 is a square exactly when the two tangent directions are rational. The unit-c4 minimality obstruction and the split/nonsplit I_n Tamagawa formulas apply. The T2 test suite cross-checks split and nonsplit examples with PARI and direct point counts.

This does not permit the p>=5 short-form preprocessing at 3: division by 3 would not be a unit change. T2 still rejects p=3; a separately audited T3/general-model extension must implement that proof.
