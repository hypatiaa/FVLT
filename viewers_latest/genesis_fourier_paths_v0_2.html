Path formula and exact weights

Each of the k entries ranges over every residue modulo q. For Q = x₁x₂ + ⋯ + xₖ₋₁xₖ, Cᵣ sums the tuple weights ∏ t(xⱼ) with Q ≡ r. The pen draws Cᵣ exp(2πihr/q) in ascending residue order. The zero residue has gcd q and conductor 1.

If Cᵣ is constant on an orbit, its contribution is exactly that weight times the Ramanujan sum. Otherwise the viewer adds the individually weighted arrows and displays a numerical approximation.
