Dial ledger

Lift 0 is the flat pen drawing. In the pen frame the ring has radius √X, or √q when X = q, and sits at the pen's last height. The helix frame uses one fixed map, T(z) = (z + w)/(q·w) with w = ζa/(ζa − 1). It sends the comb exactly onto Γ_q: step n becomes ζa·n/q at height n/q, and the ring is the cylinder of radius 1/q. Lift 0.25 gives true proportions there.

Readout

The endpoint is Σ_k c_k·ζa·k, with c_k the exact integer sum of w(n) over n ≡ k (mod q). The decimals are only for display.

FVLT pen, as in FVLT.PenGaussV1.pen: steps phase(2·a·n/q), weights w(n). For the Legendre weight with X = q and q prime, the endpoint is the Gauss sum, which ends exactly √q from the start (Lean B4, bus #2227/#2229). For μ at a = 0 the pen walks the real axis and ends at the Mertens value M(X). The sign overlay is Astra's control from bus #2231.
