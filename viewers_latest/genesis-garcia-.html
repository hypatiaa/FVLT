Why the twist leaves 3,100 rows

At X = 100,000,000, the radius is 10,000. One interval has 6,107,381 negative rows with only 6,106,106 positive rows in reach. It strands 1,275 negatives. The band already has 550 more positives, leaving 550 + 2 × 1,275 = 3,100 rows. A radius of 14,207 pairs every negative row at this cutoff.
