Reserve branch v0.2 · drawing register, exact headings q(k) mod 10080

What it is (v0.5.2). Two kinds of exact walks. Clip walks are the closed Eisenstein walks that bound the bird, its five tone bands, the eye, the enclosed paper holes, and (new in v0.5–v0.5.2) the luminous feather-margin, toe-scute center, 7-claw sickle gloss/halftone, and beak-commissure highlight masks (layer 10); they are certified closed (integer check) and are never drawn, they only say where tone strokes may go. Ink lines are open fine walks of cyclotomic phasors (unit teeth ζ^q, integer q, 10080 directions): the smooth hard edges (beak ridge, mandible, 7 sickle talons, branch), upper-wing covert creases, beak commissure cleft, transverse toe scute rings, and craggy bark crevices. Feathered edges get no outline: feather tips taper into the paper. In v0.5.2, all 7 claws have anatomically fitted sickle silhouettes with continuous 2 px dorsal specular highlight strips and unbroken adjacent tree bark grain, dark tone strokes (L2–L4) are clipped out of the luminous feather margins while L0–L1 shade smoothly across them, and L4 / head ink depth matches the dark velvety contrast of the source drawing.

Why it matters. Closure of the clip walks is certified in exact integer arithmetic, every phasor is an integer phase, and the file is small. Where the tone strokes are placed is a drawing choice made in floating point (correction v0.4.3 / v0.5.1, after Astra #426).

Credits. Instrument: mcomb (cyclotomic phasors). Curlicue & v0.5 feather-shingle/scute/talon architecture: Gemini (vision seat). Baseline program (v0.4.2): Opus (Claude Opus 5.5).

v0.5.3: the reserve branch. After the base drawing finishes (s = 1), the pen goes on through three layers drawn natively from the branch's stroke data, in painter order: the white reserve (2,142 strokes), the black cross-hatch halftone (177) and the white glint tips (576). Every branch stroke is an open comb arc: q(k) = q0 + k·b + ⌊a·k(k−1)/(2d)⌋ with d = 4, heading 2π q(k)/10080, unit steps. The headings are exact integers; the start points, nib widths, the tapered nib profile, the clip (beak, legs and claws, branch wood and eye kept untouched) and the rasterization are drawing register. Use the layer switches, “Base only” and “Base + branch” to compare, and zoom (wheel, pinch or the buttons; drag to pan; double-click or Fit to see the whole picture). Exports show the whole picture with the layers that are on, never the pen.

Credits (v0.5.3). Base viewer and raven drawing (v0.5.2): Gemini (vision seat). The reserve-layer idea, the cross-hatch-as-halftone idea and the request for this viewer: saphir. Reserve branch v0.2 and this viewer: Claude Opus 5.5.

Limits. The tracing is numerical (thresholds, multi-scale difference of Gaussians, thinning, structure tensor). Spacing, alpha, width and the extra cross-hatching and highlight families are drawing-register choices.

Tracing: numerical (from the drawing). Phasors, line vertices, closure and tone-stroke shapes: exact integers in ℤ[ζ₆] and ℤ[ζ₁₀₀₈₀]. Tone-stroke placement and the drawing itself: floating point (drawing register).
