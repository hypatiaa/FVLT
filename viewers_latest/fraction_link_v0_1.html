Fraction Link

v0.1 · 2026-11-01 · One fraction, four realizations. Pick a fraction once (tap any panel or a chip) and every view highlights where it lives: the helix family seen from above, the exponential lift (FVLT), the Cayley disk, and the divisor atom Div(N). Idea from Codex's note on saphir's helix screenshot; the views are saphir's. Exact register for every address and identity below; drawing register for pixels only.

Helix family, top view

Ring n has radius 1/n; bead j sits at ζnj. A fraction a/q occurs on every ring n = kq as bead j = ka (all ringed). Straight chords: det-1 contacts (adjacency, never incidence).

Exponential lift (FVLT)

w = e2πiz: cusp at the centre, a/q at ζqa, Ford discs as ovals, Farey edges as petals and one spoke. Kisses at e−2π/m·ζmn.

Cayley disk

w = (z − i)/(z + i): centre i, cusp at 1, a/q at the Pythagorean point ((a² − q²), −2aq)/(a² + q²). Farey edges are circles orthogonal to the rim; translates |k| ≤ 3 faint.

Atom Div(N)

Each prime power pa has cyclic order a + 1; exponent e gives the phase e/(a + 1). A divisor carries a/q when one of its exponents reduces to it. Bars: exponent lattice per prime, carriers marked.
